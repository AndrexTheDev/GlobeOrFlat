Every record below was captured by the GlobeOrFlat Android sensor-fusion client and bound to a hardware-backed ECDSA P-256 device signature. Raw telemetry is write-once storage; the ledger is append-only. The same data is rendered on the 3D globe and on the flat model's azimuthal-equidistant plane — judge both.

SCIENTIFIC DISCLAIMER

Consumer sensors carry real tolerances and atmospheric refraction varies around standard k=0.14. Single measurements are evidence with uncertainty — cite spreads, not anecdotes. Not certified for navigation, surveying, engineering or legal use.

SAFETY

Never operate the app while driving. Watch your surroundings near cliffs, water and traffic. The horizon is not worth your life.

API ENDPOINT

Base URL of the Cloudflare Worker (globeorflat-api). The hub appends /api/v1/measurements. Saved to this browser; the ?api= URL parameter always wins. Leave empty to use same-origin (works when this portal is deployed as a Worker static asset next to the API).

Ion tokens are per-account secrets — this one is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never committed.
